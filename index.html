04 / POWER & LABOR

More access. New inequalities.

01 / ACCESS

Who gained power?

Computing and publishing became accessible to more people. Institutions and companies still controlled infrastructure, software and access; equipment costs and digital skills excluded many communities.

A simple example

Two students can have the same ability, but the one with a computer and a connection has more chances to practise.

02 / WORK

How did work change?

Digital tools changed clerical tasks and created demand for programmers and technical support. Productivity gains came with retraining needs, job restructuring and greater capacity to monitor workers.

A simple example

A spreadsheet can speed up office calculations. Workers also need training to use it, and some tasks change or disappear.
